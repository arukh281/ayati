## 7. NEW — BUYER JOURNEY SECTION

> **Add this section after "Proof of Delivery."**
> **Psychology:** Buyers are anxious about the unknown. A clear 4-step process removes that anxiety and makes taking the first step feel safe.

### Section Heading
`What happens after you reach out`

### Section Subheading
`No pressure. No script. Just a clear path from interest to keys.`

### The 4 Steps

**Step 1: Book a site visit**
We confirm within a few hours and send you location details. You pick the time that works.

**Step 2: Walk the actual build**
You see the site as it stands — not a showroom, not renders on a screen. Someone who knows every material choice and construction decision walks with you.

**Step 3: Review floor plans and pricing**
Full documentation of what you're getting and what you're paying. Take it home. Share it with your family. There is no pressure to decide on the day.

**Step 4: Book and track your home**
Transparent payment stages linked to construction milestones. A team you can reach by call or WhatsApp at any point. No going silent after booking.

## 10. NEW — FAQ SECTION

> **Add this section after the Founder section.**
> **Psychology:** Unanswered questions don't disappear — buyers take them to competitors. A visible FAQ keeps people on your site longer, addresses fears before they become objections, and pre-qualifies serious buyers.

### Section Heading
`Questions buyers ask us`

---

**Q: How do I know the price I'm quoted is what I'll actually pay?**
A: It's in writing before you sign. Your booking agreement documents the full price with no additional charges at possession — no floor-rise fees, no hidden amenity costs. Ask to see this at your first meeting.

---

**Q: What is the payment plan after booking?**
A: Payment is linked to construction milestones — not arbitrary demands. The full structure is shared in writing at your first meeting, before you're asked to commit to anything.

---

**Q: Which banks have approved your projects for home loans?**
A: ICICI Bank, Bajaj Finserv, Piramal Finance, Aditya Birla Capital, Aavas, GIC Housing Finance, Adhaar Housing Finance, and SMFG India Credit. Banks conduct their own due diligence on a developer and a specific project before approving home loans — their approval is an independent verification of the project's standing.

---

**Q: What are the possession timelines?**
A: Timelines vary by project and are shared clearly at your first meeting. They are documented in your agreement — not given verbally and forgotten. [Ayati team: if you can publish specific dates per project, do it here.]

---

**Q: Can I visit the construction site before I decide?**
A: Yes — and we'd encourage it. You'll walk the actual site with someone from our team who knows every detail of the build. Not a showroom. Not a presentation. The real thing.

---

**Q: I've been cheated by a builder before. Why should I trust Ayati?**
A: That's the most important question you can ask, and we'd rather earn that trust than just ask for it. Start here: we've told you upfront that our projects are RERA-exempt — most builders in our position would stay quiet about that. We've also told you exactly why. Beyond that: visit an ongoing site and see the construction yourself. Ask to speak with a family who has already received possession from us. Check that 8 independent lending institutions — ICICI, Bajaj Finserv, Aditya Birla, and others — have approved our projects after their own due diligence. And ask to see your full booking agreement before signing anything. If anything is unclear, we'd rather you take more time than feel pressured.

---

**Q: Are there customisation options for the home?**
A: Depending on the construction stage when you book, we may be able to accommodate changes to finishes and fittings. This is discussed at your first meeting once we understand your requirements.

**Q: What happens if possession is delayed?**
A: Possession timelines are written into your agreement. If there is a delay, we communicate proactively — not reactively. [Ayati team: if you have a specific delay policy or compensation clause, add it here. Transparency on this point is a strong differentiator.]
