The Strategic Advantage of Living in Central Noida: Sectors 73 & 75

By Ayati Group 5 min read Noida · Location · Investment

Sectors 73 and 75 sit in the middle of Noida with direct access to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway and the FNG Expressway, and the Sector 62 office hubs are typically about a 15-minute drive in light traffic. For a family with one commute into Delhi and another into Greater Noida, that position is the argument.

Ayati Group builds in this location deliberately. Here's what we've observed, and what buyers consistently tell us matters most.

How well connected are Sectors 73 and 75?

Sectors 73 and 75 connect directly to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway and the FNG Expressway, and sit close to the Noida Sector 18 metro station on the Blue Line. The links that matter day to day:

  • Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, direct access to Delhi, Greater Noida, and the Yamuna Expressway corridor
  • FNG Expressway, connecting Faridabad, Noida, and Ghaziabad without entering Delhi
  • Proximity to Noida Sector 18 Metro Station, Blue Line with direct connectivity to Delhi and Vaishali
  • Proposed metro extension, planned infrastructure that will further improve public transit access

For families with working members commuting to Delhi, Gurugram, or Greater Noida, this connectivity significantly reduces daily travel time.

"Central Noida's expressway access and metro proximity make it one of the few NCR locations where you're genuinely close to everything without paying South Delhi or Golf Course Road prices."

How far is Sector 73 from Noida's office hubs?

From Sector 73, the major Noida office hubs in Sector 62 are typically about a 15-minute drive in light traffic, and Sector 18, one of NCR's most active commercial zones, is the nearest large retail and business district.

Prominent IT parks and corporate offices sit in the same commercial belt, which is why the sector suits professionals who want a short commute.

What schools and hospitals are near Sector 73?

Multiple CBSE and ICSE schools sit within a 3–5 km radius, and multispecialty hospitals, diagnostic centres and clinics are a short drive away. The larger institutions nearby:

  • Amity University, one of India's largest private universities, about a 20-minute drive away
  • Gautam Buddha University, Greater Noida campus with strong academic programmes
  • Multiple reputed CBSE and ICSE schools within a 3–5 km radius

Healthcare facilities, including multispecialty hospitals, diagnostic centres, and clinics, are easily accessible, ensuring comprehensive care without long travel times.

Recreational and Lifestyle Amenities

Living in Central Noida means enjoying a vibrant lifestyle. Sectors 73 and 75 are surrounded by:

  • Parks and open green spaces within walking distance
  • Shopping complexes and neighbourhood markets for daily convenience
  • Restaurants, cafés, and entertainment options in the Sector 18 hub
  • Upcoming infrastructure projects that promise to further elevate the quality of urban living

Investment Potential

The strategic location of Sectors 73 and 75 has attracted consistent real estate development. The combination of good connectivity, proximity to commercial hubs, strong infrastructure, and a high quality of life makes these sectors a promising investment destination within the NCR.

Property values in Central Noida have historically appreciated steadily, driven by the expressway corridor and the ongoing development of the wider Noida ecosystem.

Should you buy in Noida or Greater Noida?

Buy in Sector 73 if your daily journeys point towards Delhi and the Noida office sectors: from there the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway is about 10 minutes away, and Botanical Garden metro and Fortis Hospital in Sector 62 about 15. Buy on the Dadri Surajpur Road side of Greater Noida if your journeys point towards Pari Chowk, about 15 minutes away, with daily retail at Haldoni Mod about 5 and the expressway about 15. Sector 18 is roughly 20 minutes from either. These times are rounded up from mapped driving routes in light traffic.

Ayati Group builds in both. Aadya Homes and Aadya Residency are in Sector 73; Ayati Greens 1 and Ayati Greens 2 are on Dadri Surajpur Road.

Why Sector 73 Specifically

Within Central Noida, Sector 73 offers a particular advantage: it sits on the expressway-facing side with good access to both the FNG corridor and the Sector 18 commercial zone, while being sufficiently internal to remain quieter than the busiest arterial sectors.

For a family with commutes into both Delhi and Greater Noida, yes. Sectors 73 and 75 give you expressway and metro access, Sector 62 offices about fifteen minutes away, and CBSE and ICSE schools inside a 3–5 km radius.
